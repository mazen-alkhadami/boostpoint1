Signage & Advertising Boards

Design, fabrication, and installation of high-impact 3D commercial building signage and illuminated channel letters.

Ready to elevate your brand's reach? The media and advertising specialists at BoostPoint deliver high-impact advertising boards designed to captivate your target audience and make your message impossible to ignore. We engineer 3D channel letters, pylon signs, and illuminated facades that meet Saudi Building Code requirements.

Capabilities

  • 3D Channel letters crafted from stainless steel, aluminum, and cast acrylic
  • Halo backlit and front-lit LED storefront facades and corporate headers
  • Monolithic pylon signs and directional totems for commercial compounds
  • Illuminated lightboxes for medical centers, pharmacies, and restaurants
  • Premium interior 3D reception lobby logos and architectural branding

Advantages

  • Unforgettable 24/7 visual presence establishing market credibility
  • High-efficiency, long-lifespan commercial LED modules with low power draw
  • Corrosion-resistant alloys built to withstand coastal humidity in Dammam and Khobar
  • Full compliance with Eastern Province Municipality guidelines and safety codes

Applications

  • Corporate headquarters, banking branches, and hospital facades
  • Shopping malls, commercial complexes, and retail centers
  • Factories, logistics hubs, and industrial warehouses in Dammam & Jubail

Specifications

  • Internal structural frames reinforced with hot-dip galvanized steel
  • Weatherproof IP67/IP68 certified LED transformers and solid-state modules
  • Finishes: mirror-polished stainless, brushed hairline, matte black, and titanium gold
